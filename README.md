# LUDWIG WITTGENSTEIN

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **LOGIKĒ**.

LUDWIG WITTGENSTEIN (1889–1951) — LOGIKĒ, the modern foundations; [[bertrand-russell]]'s most famous student. In the TRACTATUS LOGICO-PHILOSOPHICUS (1921) he gave the TRUTH TABLE its modern form and showed a statement's meaning is NOTHING BUT how its truth depends on its parts — and from that, that the truths of LOGIC are TAUTOLOGIES (true on EVERY row, no matter how the world is) → logic says nothing about reality, it shows the SCAFFOLDING; what can't be pictured (ethics, the mystical) lies outside what language can say ('whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent'). LIVE: a truth-table machine — build a propositional formula over p,q,r + connectives, the full table is computed and classified TAUTOLOGY / CONTRADICTION / CONTINGENT (verified p∨¬p=taut, p∧¬p=contradiction, De Morgan & the contrapositive = tautologies). CONFIDENCE-TIERED: STRONG (the truth-table method is standard & correct; 'logical truths are contentless tautologies' is a genuine clarification of what logic IS — the great-grandparent of the SAT solver) / NOT-THE-SOLE-INVENTOR (⚠ truth tables appear near-simultaneously in Wittgenstein, [[emil-post]] 1921, and earlier in [[charles-peirce]]; his move was philosophical) / HE-TOOK-IT-BACK (⚠ he later REPUDIATED much of the Tractatus in the Philosophical Investigations — meaning is use, not picturing; and he's a PHILOSOPHER first). The man: a steel heir who gave away a fortune, wrote it partly in WWI trenches, quit to teach schoolchildren, then returned to demolish his own system. [[emil-post]] ← · next → George Boolos.
